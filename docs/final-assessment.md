# Assessment after source reconciliation — 9 November 2026

All 41 missing financial quarters have been reconciled against original exchange
or issuer quarterly statements. Coverage is now 360/360 required cells and 100%
of 695 historical signal dates pass the unchanged financial-data thresholds.
The earlier blocked audit remains archived under
`financial_pilot_v1/before_reconciliation/`; its original 53 XML rejections remain
visible. The independent PDF reviews do not repair malformed XML.

The subsequent [controlled financial experiment](verified-pilot-experiment.md)
is also complete. Its financial-feature candidate failed the frozen validation
rule. Later retail losses were smaller, but top-five selection did not improve
consistently. No stronger predictor is established and no production model was
replaced. The earlier finding that a reliable trading edge
has not been demonstrated still stands.

The frozen paper observer has collected completed quotes through9 November 2026.
One cohort is recorded and none has matured. It awaits entry after its9 November
publication and then its fixed21-session evaluation. One provider quote error
(EMBASSY.NS) remains visible. Existing predictions, feature snapshots, weights and
trading rules retain their original hashes.

Future pipeline runs invoke the independent outcome observer before new model
scoring. It can also be run with `bash scripts/paper_outcomes.sh`. This remains an
on-demand application; no background scheduler or deployment was created.

See [the financial pilot](financial-pilot.md) for the before/after audit, original
source review policy and commands, and [the information trial](information-research.md)
for prospective evaluation rules.

A subsequent [target, abstention and horizon experiment](decision-experiment.md)
is also complete. Neither horizon qualified. Selective sector-relative signals
reduced drawdown; the 21-session result failed an exposure-matched momentum
comparison and the 63-session variant has too few independent cycles. Historical
consensus data remain unavailable in the repository, so the surprise arm was not
run. No production or existing paper model changed.
